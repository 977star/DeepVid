# DeepVid · 知影

Turn audio and video into illustrated notes you can read, search, and reuse.

DeepVid offers illustrated deep reading, chapter overviews, mindmaps, and contextual Q&A. Connect AI assistants through MCP or export notes to Obsidian. It supports online and local models, with offline speech recognition on your device.

**[Visit the official website for features and usage guides →](https://deepvid.977star.com)**

## Download

This repository is an official distribution channel for DeepVid installers and release notes.
